# PMC5649675 Source Notes

Source URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5649675/

Access note: Full text was available through NCBI PMC EFetch XML on 2026-05-17.

Rankable extraction boundary:
- Included initial fever syndrome, urinary/chest findings, CT adenopathy, inflammatory labs, negative infectious workup, initial imaging, non-diagnostic lymph node and marrow workup, transfer labs, ferritin, arthralgias, and hypoalbuminemia.
- Did not put the final bladder pathology or late ICU course into rankable observations.

Record-only boundary:
- Working rheumatologic diagnosis, immunosuppressive treatment, late hematuria/hydronephrosis/ICU deterioration, final pathology, and death were kept in `record_only`.

Caveat:
- Several observations are temporally staged; entries through transfer/early tertiary workup were treated as pre-diagnostic, while later decompensation was record-only.
