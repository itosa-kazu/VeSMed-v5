## Rankable extraction basis
- Used first presentation/reassessment, early CT and labs, and pre-diagnostic follow-up workup that established recurrent thrombosis, portal hypertension, ascites, coagulation abnormalities, and anticardiolipin positivity.
- Prior mesenteric venous thrombosis and bowel resection are risk/history context, not final labels.

## Record-only separation
- Named final diagnosis, anticoagulation and other treatments, surgical referral, and late complications are record_only.
