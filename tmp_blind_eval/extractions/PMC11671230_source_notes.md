# PMC11671230 Source Notes

Source URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11671230/

Access note: Full text was available through NCBI PMC EFetch XML on 2026-05-17.

Rankable extraction boundary:
- Included presentation symptoms, physical examination, CBC/LDH, tumor lysis absence, smear/marrow/flow findings, neurologic/visual symptoms, and retinal findings.
- Therapy resistance, leukocytapheresis response, molecular treatment adaptation, remission status, and transplant planning were kept record-only.

Record-only boundary:
- Diagnostic labels, cytogenetic/molecular interpretation in the treatment course, chemotherapy/leukocytapheresis/TKI treatment, and follow-up response were kept in `record_only`.

Caveat:
- Some immunophenotyping markers are highly specific but were included as objective pre-diagnostic workup, without adding disease labels to rankable observations.
