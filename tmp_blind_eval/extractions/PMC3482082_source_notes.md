## Snapshot Boundary

I treated hospital day 32 as the diagnostic snapshot because the case presentation describes sudden headache, visual disturbance, three generalized tonic-clonic convulsions, postictal confusion, high fever, blood pressure 180/90 mm Hg, lumbar puncture results, and same-day brain MRI findings at that point.

Rankable observations include diagnosis-neutral clinical facts before or at that snapshot:

- Demographics and relevant prior history.
- Symptoms, vitals, physical exam, labs, urinalysis, imaging, EEG, CSF, serology, microbiology, and observed bleeding/vascular findings.
- The day 32 MRI observation as a neutral radiologic finding only.
