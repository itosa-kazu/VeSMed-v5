## Rankable Evidence Pulled

- Demographics: 15-year-old male patient.
- Exposure context: home water-heater-related carbon monoxide exposure, estimated 6-8 hours.
- Initial presentation: reduced consciousness, coma, urinary incontinence, cyanosis, shallow/weak breathing, hypoxemia, hypotension.
- Initial ABG: pH 7.3, PaCO2 31.8 mmHg, PaO2 40.9 mmHg, HCO3 16 mEq/L, SaO2 72%, SBE -8.5 mEq/L.
- Imaging: brain edema on CT; bilateral pulmonary edema on chest x-ray; later worsening pulmonary edema and increasing bilateral alveolar consolidation.
- Exposure marker: arterial COHb 51.9% within 1 hour after discovery; 8.4% four hours after admission.
- Labs: ammonia 103 ug/dL, Na 141 mEq/L, K 3.8 mEq/L, Ca 9.9 mg/dL, glucose 198 mg/dL, WBC 17,490/uL, Hb 16.3 g/dL, platelets 336,000/uL, cardiac troponin I 4.19 ng/mL with normal upper limit <0.4 ng/mL.
- Other findings: fresh blood and pink foamy sputum from the endotracheal tube; ejection fraction 55%; severe hypoxemia later after admission.
