# PMC3105562 Source Notes

Source URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3105562/

Access note: Full text was available through NCBI PMC EFetch XML on 2026-05-17.

Rankable extraction boundary:
- Included admission mental status, agitation, vomiting, tremor, urine/appetite symptoms, vitals, hepatomegaly, initial liver/lipid labs, negative viral screens, ultrasound, and early delirium onset.
- Sedative dosing and response were not used as rankable evidence.

Record-only boundary:
- Thiamine/dextrose, benzodiazepines, propofol, intubation/mechanical ventilation, RASS monitoring, extubation, discharge, and rehabilitation referral were kept in `record_only`.

Caveat:
- The article table with sedative doses was not needed for rankable extraction and was treated as treatment-course data.
