  - mechanism_audit_origin edges are registry/audit evidence only and are not used as scoring gates.
  - Pair candidates are two-disease vector-field superpositions, not labels emitted by the LLM.
  - Opposing disease forces are combined in standardized axis space so a strong TTP platelet-consumption field is not canceled by weak AOSD reactive thrombocytosis.
  - OOD/null-model check reports logP_best_known - logP_background_only; it deliberately does not apply a hard unknown-disease threshold.
  - Culture/ADAMTS13-confirmatory axes are allowed if present in the case JSON; remove them from case evidence if testing presentation-only diagnosis.
