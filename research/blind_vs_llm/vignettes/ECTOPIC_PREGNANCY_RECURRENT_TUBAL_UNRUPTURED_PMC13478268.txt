A 30-year-old woman presented with an 8-day history of right-sided lower abdominal pain and mild vaginal spotting. Her last menstrual period was 8 weeks earlier. She had a history of left salpingectomy for tubal ectopic pregnancy 3 years ago. She had no history of intrauterine device use or assisted reproductive technology, and she is a non-smoker.

On admission:
- Blood pressure 109/70 mmHg, heart rate 81/min, respiratory rate 20/min, temperature 36.2 °C
- Abdomen soft, without guarding or rebound tenderness
- Gynecological examination: slight bleeding from the uterine cavity; no pain on cervical motion; uterus normal size and non-tender; right adnexa thickened but non-tender; left adnexa normal

Transvaginal ultrasound: no gestational sac in the uterine cavity (endometrial thickness 10.4 mm); a 13 × 10 mm mixed-echo lesion adjacent to the right ovary with a small hypoechoic area and abundant peripheral blood flow on color Doppler; a possible corpus luteum (18 × 12 mm) in the right ovary (O-RADS 2); no pelvic free fluid.

Laboratory tests on admission:
- Serum β-hCG 5,823 IU/L
- Hemoglobin 119 g/L
- ALT 10.6 U/L, AST 17 U/L
