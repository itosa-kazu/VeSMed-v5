An 11-year-old girl (body weight 31.2 kg) is admitted with 1 month of decreased exercise tolerance with dyspnea on exertion, accompanied by left lower abdominal pain.

History: At a nearby hospital, an echocardiogram showed moderate tricuspid regurgitation with a peak velocity of 3.56 m/s and an estimated pulmonary artery pressure of about 50 mm Hg. She was treated with bosentan for 1 month, but her symptoms worsened and she was referred. She has no prior medical history, no trauma or surgery, no family history of genetic disorders, and no drug or toxin exposure.

Examination on admission: Temperature 37.3 °C, pulse 138/min, respiratory rate 24/min, blood pressure 92/61 mm Hg. Heart sounds are strong, with a particularly loud second heart sound in the pulmonary area. The liver and spleen are enlarged and non-tender. An eczema-like rash is present on the left lower limb. No edema of the lower limbs.

Cardiopulmonary evaluation:
- Echocardiography: moderate tricuspid regurgitation, velocity 3.56 m/s, estimated pulmonary artery pressure 50.7 mm Hg; right atrial and right ventricular enlargement
- Right heart catheterization: right atrial pressure 14 mm Hg; pulmonary artery pressure 81/56 mm Hg (mean 67 mm Hg)
- Contrast CT of the pulmonary arteries and ventilation/perfusion scan: dilated pulmonary artery; no filling defects or obstructive pulmonary arterial lesions
- Cardiac MRI: pulmonary artery dilation, right atrial and right ventricular enlargement, normal right ventricular systolic function

Laboratory results after admission:
- White blood cells 4.81 x 10^9/L; hemoglobin 120 g/L; platelets 98 x 10^9/L
- CRP 10 mg/L (reference 0-8); ESR 29 mm/h (reference 0-20)
- NT-proBNP 96 pg/mL (reference 0-250)
- AST 23 U/L; ALT 36 U/L; total bilirubin 7.7 umol/L; albumin 32.0 g/L (reference 35-55)
- Creatinine <29 umol/L (within reference range)
