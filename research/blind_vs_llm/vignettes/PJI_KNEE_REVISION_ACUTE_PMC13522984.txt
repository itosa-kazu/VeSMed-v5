History: He has had multiple surgeries on the left knee, most recently a revision left total knee arthroplasty 2 years ago.

Examination: He is afebrile (other vital signs not reported). The left knee is painful, swollen, and warm.

Investigations on admission:
- Left knee aspiration: white blood cells 107,500 cells/uL with 87% polymorphonuclear neutrophils; red blood cells 16,000 cells/uL; leukocyte esterase 3+
- Erythrocyte sedimentation rate 25 mm/h
- C-reactive protein 143.7 mg/L
