A 46-year-old Japanese man presented with epigastric pain that had started six days earlier. He had no significant past medical history. He had no history of habitual alcohol consumption (occasional drinker only) and was not taking any regular medications, over-the-counter drugs, or herbal supplements. No vital signs were reported.

Initial blood tests (reference ranges in parentheses):
- Amylase 173 U/L (44–132)
- Lipase 485 U/L (13–55)
- Total protein 7.6 g/dL (6.6–8.1); albumin 4.6 g/dL (4.1–5.1)
- Total bilirubin 0.5 mg/dL (0.4–1.5)
- AST 16 U/L (13–30); ALT 14 U/L (10–42); ALP 67 U/L (38–113); GGT 30 U/L (13–64)
- WBC 7.5 × 10^3/µL (3.3–8.6)
- CRP 0.21 mg/dL (< 0.14)

Abdominal MRI at presentation: a 4 × 2 cm mass in the pancreatic body with high signal intensity on diffusion-weighted imaging and a corresponding reduction in the apparent diffusion coefficient, with mild dilation of the distal main pancreatic duct.
