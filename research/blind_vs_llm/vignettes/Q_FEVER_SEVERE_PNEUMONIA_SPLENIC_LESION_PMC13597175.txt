A 64-year-old man living in metropolitan Cairns (Far North Queensland, Australia) presented to hospital with 2 days of confusion. He is a smoker. Two weeks before presentation he buried a dead bandicoot that he had found in his garden, wearing gloves but no face mask.

On admission:
- Temperature 38.7 °C
- Blood pressure 88/50 mmHg (hypotensive)
- Hypoxic respiratory failure: oxygen saturation 92% on high-flow nasal oxygen with FiO2 0.45 delivered at 40 L/min

Laboratory tests on admission:
- Platelets 51 × 10^9/L (reference 140–400)
- Creatinine 246 µmol/L (reference 36–73); acute kidney injury
- Total bilirubin 36 µmol/L (reference < 20)
- ALT 101 U/L (reference < 45)
- AST 337 U/L (reference < 35)

CT on admission: bilateral bronchopneumonic changes; hepatomegaly; splenomegaly with a 3.9 cm lesion in the inferior pole of the spleen.
