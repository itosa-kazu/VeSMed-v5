A 9-year-old girl (weight 25 kg) living in Pakistan was admitted to a tertiary care hospital.

History: For about 1-2 months before admission she had a relapsing pattern of intermittent fever. For more than 2 weeks she has had persistent high-grade fever (104 F / 40.0 C) with cough, poor oral intake, fatigue, and pallor. Before transfer she had received 2 weeks of empirical intravenous antibiotics (meropenem, piperacillin-tazobactam, linezolid, and vancomycin), and the fever continued despite this.

Examination: Temperature 104 F (40.0 C). Conscious but irritable. Pallor. Mild pedal edema. Liver palpable 3-4 cm and spleen palpable 4-5 cm below the costal margin. Bilateral crepitations on chest auscultation, no clinical pleural effusion. Neurological examination unremarkable. No rash and no arthritis.

Laboratory results on hospital day 1:
- Hemoglobin 8.4 g/dL; hematocrit 26.2%; RBC 3.93 x10^12/L
- MCV 66.7 fL; MCH 21.4 pg; MCHC 32.1 g/dL; RDW 22.3%
- WBC 8.5 x10^9/L: neutrophils 7.3% (absolute about 0.62 x10^9/L), lymphocytes 39.2% (about 3.33 x10^9/L), monocytes 53.4% (about 4.54 x10^9/L), eosinophils 0.0%, basophils 0.1%
- Platelets 114 x10^9/L
- Triglycerides 349 mg/dL
- Fibrinogen 107 mg/dL
- Ferritin 1144 ng/mL
- Albumin 2.3 g/dL
- ALT 70 U/L; AST 74 U/L; alkaline phosphatase 239 U/L; total bilirubin 1.1 mg/dL
- Hyponatremia was noted (value not reported)

Imaging:
- Abdominal ultrasound: hepatosplenomegaly with mild pericholecystic free fluid and mild pelvic free fluid.
- Chest X-ray (PA): normal lung fields, no cardiomegaly, no pleural effusion, no mediastinal widening.
- Contrast-enhanced brain MRI (obtained for fever and irritability): mildly increased intra- and extra-axial CSF spaces with prominent ventricles and sulci (mild atrophic changes); mucoperiosteal thickening of the bilateral maxillary sinuses, ethmoid air cells, and sphenoid sinus.
